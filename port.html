About

From AI fundamentals to systems engineering.

My path started with AI and machine learning fundamentals, then moved through deep learning and NLP into generative AI and large language models. Working on real LLM applications shifted my focus from the models themselves to the engineering problems around them — grounding, retrieval, orchestration, agents, evaluation, latency, and reliability.

That shift is what pulled me toward LLM engineering and AI systems engineering: fine-tuning models for specific domains, building retrieval pipelines, designing multi-agent workflows, and wrapping all of it in the infrastructure that keeps an AI system predictable under real conditions.

My focus is not just making AI work once. It's understanding what happens when the system needs to work repeatedly, reliably, and under real constraints.

Engineering Philosophy

Six principles I build against.

01Reliability over demosA system should continue behaving predictably when things go wrong.
02Architecture over promptsGood AI products require systems, not only better prompts.
03Evaluation over assumptionsAI behavior should be measured and tested.
04Observability over guessingWhen an AI system fails, you should know why.
05Modularity over monolithsModels, retrieval, tools, gateways, and validation should be replaceable.
06Production mindsetThink about failure modes before they happen.
